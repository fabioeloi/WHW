# WHY — Hello Wave

## Purpose

Teach the WHW loop in 15 minutes on a trivial product (a greeter), so the
mechanics — queue, claim, evidence, gates, close — are learned before they
matter.

## Non-goals

- Not a real product. No users, no deploys, no roadmap.
- Not the full process reference (that lives in the WHW repo's `docs/`).

## Principles

- Every command in the walkthrough is real and re-runnable.
- The wave ends with proof (checkpoints, report, metrics), not claims.
